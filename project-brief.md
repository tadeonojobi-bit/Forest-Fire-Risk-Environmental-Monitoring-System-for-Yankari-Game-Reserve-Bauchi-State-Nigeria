# My Project Brief

## The Question
Which zones of Yankari Game Reserve carry the highest wildfire risk in dry season, and does that match where fires have actually been detected?

## The data I need
- Reserve boundary — WDPA (Protected Planet) — https://www.protectedplanet.net — Shapefile/GeoPackage - 1.9MB
- Imagery for NDVI — Landsat 8/9 — https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov — GeoTIFF (bands 4 & 5) - 1.05GB
- Elevation/slope — SRTM DEM — https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov — GeoTIFF - 25MB
- Active fire detections — NASA FIRMS — https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/map — Shapefile/CSV
- Precipitation — GRIDMET - https://app.climateengine.org/climateEngine — GeoTIFF
- Weather (wind/temp/humidity) — NASA POWER — https://power.larc.nasa.gov/data-access-viewer — CSV
